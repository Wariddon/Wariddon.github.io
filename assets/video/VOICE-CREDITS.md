# Career film narration

The two English films use a synthetic narrator reading a first-person career script. The voice is not a recording of Wariddon. Speech was generated locally and edited into the animated cards with pauses between phrases. No model weights, dataset recordings or sample clips are distributed in this site.

- **English** — Piper, en_US-ljspeech-medium. The publisher identifies LJ Speech as its public-domain training dataset. [Voice model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium/MODEL_CARD) · [LJ Speech dataset provenance](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/)

The film visuals use original typography, shapes and work descriptions. The English editions run for 45 seconds and 141.5 seconds.
